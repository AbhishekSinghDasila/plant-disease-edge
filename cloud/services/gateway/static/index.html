How is this measured?
No real soil probe is connected yet. The value comes from the soil-sim container on the laptop: a small model of a pot drying out (faster when hot or dry air), running 60× faster than real time (1 simulated hour per minute) and sending plant/soil every 30 s.
Real sensor (sketch ready, not wired yet): a capacitive soil probe in the pot, wired to the NodeMCU's A0 pin (iot/nodemcu_leaf_node). The NodeMCU averages 10 readings of the raw value (≈820 in dry air, ≈380 in water), converts it to 0–100 % and publishes the same plant/soil message every 30 s. The simulator notices the real readings and pauses by itself, so nothing else has to change.

How is watering decided and detected?
Needs water? The irrigation container compares the soil % with the band for the crop (e.g. tomato: dry below 40 %, wilting below 25 %, keep watering up to 65 %). The crop comes from the latest confident diagnosis. Hot or dry air (≥ 32 °C or VPD ≥ 1.6 kPa) raises the urgency, and the 2-hour trend gives “dry in ~N h”.
Watering detected: when the soil % jumps up by 8 points or more between two readings (drying only lowers it slowly), the system records a watering, so a real probe catches watering by hand or rain too. The 💧 button below just tells the simulator that water was added. With a real probe the jump itself is detected.

    How it works: where data is measured, predicted and stored

    1 · Sensing

    • 📷 Pi Camera on the Raspberry Pi 4 real
    • 🌡 DHT11 temperature / humidity on the Pi (GPIO4) not wired yet
    • 💧 Soil moisture: soil-sim container simulated
      real probe: NodeMCU sketch ready (capacitive probe on A0); the simulator pauses by itself when it sends
    • 🔘 NodeMCU button → take photo sketch not uploaded

    2 · Prediction & decisions

    • Leaf disease: on the Pi (edge). MobileNetV2 INT8 TFLite, 38 classes / 14 crops, ~66 ms per photo, plus a photo check (blur / leaf). Works without the cloud.
    • Needs water + watering detection: irrigation container (laptop)
    • Measures (“What to do”): advisor container, knowledge base of 38 classes
    • Alerts: notifier container
    • ☁ Second opinion: cloud-inference container (laptop). The same 38-class network at full precision (FP32), shown 6 versions of the photo (mirrored, flipped, zoomed). A diagnosis is given only when edge and cloud are both sure and agree; on field photos this cut confident-wrong answers from 56 % to 30 %. A better field model with “not a leaf” and “unknown plant” classes (v2) is trained on Colab and installed with one script.

    3 · Transport

    • MQTT: Mosquitto on the Pi, bridged to Mosquitto on the laptop (topics plant/#, QoS 1)
    • Photos: HTTP upload from the Pi to /api/images
    • Offline? The Pi queues photos in captures/outbox/ and MQTT messages in the bridge, then sends them later

    4 · Storage

    • Pi: every photo + result in ~/plant_disease_edge/captures/ (log.csv)
    • Laptop: PostgreSQL 16 (Docker volume pgdata), …
    • Photos: Docker volume images, …
